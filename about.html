ABOUT US


TipToe Soles is an Australian owned and operated company. We aim to provide comfortable footwear at affordable prices to customers within Australia and around the world.

At TipToe Soles, we believe that comfortable, stylish, and affordable footwear should be accessible to everyone. Based in Sydney, Australia, we have built our reputation through years of manufacturing high-quality shoes for leading brands. Today, we are proud to launch our own range of footwear designed specifically for people who value comfort, durability, and everyday style. Our first collection focuses on daily wear and comfort shoes for adults aged 30 and above. Every pair is carefully crafted using quality materials and modern manufacturing techniques to provide excellent support, long-lasting performance, and all-day comfort.

Our Mission

To become Australia's most trusted and affordable footwear brand by delivering comfortable, high-quality shoes that fit seamlessly into everyday life.

Our Vision

To be the go-to footwear brand in Australia and around the world, recognised for comfort, quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Commitment

At TipToe Soles, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We are committed to providing reliable products, excellent service, and a seamless online shopping experience. We continually strive to improve our products and services based on customer feedback and changing market needs. Whether you're heading to work, spending time with family, or simply enjoying your day, TipToe Soles is here to keep you comfortable every step of the way.
